How the times are worked out, and what to double-check

Walk times. Straight-line distance between buildings × 1.3 for sidewalk detours, at about 3 mph. Good enough to rank options against each other; use the Google Maps links for a real routed path.

Hours and menus. Weekday serving hours come from University Housing's menu page and the Illini Union's site (fall 2026). Menus are University Housing's own daily menus.
